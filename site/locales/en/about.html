About costsimulators.com

costsimulators.com is a free collection of small cost calculators that show what everyday things really cost – from a one-hour meeting to a daily cup of coffee. No sign-up, no tracking, and every calculation runs right in your browser.

Why costsimulators.com exists

Small costs are easy to ignore. A single meeting, a coffee on the way to work or one more streaming service rarely feels expensive on its own. Add them up over a month, a year or a decade, and the numbers start to look very different.

costsimulators.com turns those everyday money questions into clear, instant answers. Each tool does one thing, asks only for the numbers it needs and shows the result immediately, so you can make better decisions about your time and money without spreadsheets or sign-ups.

The tools

  • Meeting cost calculator

    A live timer that shows what a meeting costs in real time, based on the number of participants and their hourly rate. Pause it, and change the headcount or rate mid-meeting.

  • Price in work hours

    Turn any price into the hours, days and weeks you need to work to pay for it, using your hourly, monthly or yearly pay.

  • Coffee habit calculator

    See what your coffee costs per month and over one, five and ten years.

  • Smoking cost calculator

    Find out how much money cigarettes cost you each month and over one, five and ten years.

  • Subscription cost calculator

    List streaming services, gym memberships and every other recurring payment, with weekly, monthly or yearly billing, and see the total per month, per year and over ten years.

  • Electricity cost calculator

    Work out what running a device costs per day, month and year from its wattage, how long it runs and your electricity price.

  • Trip and commute cost calculator

    Calculate the fuel cost of a trip or daily commute in kilometres and litres or miles and gallons, and split it between everyone in the car.

How the calculators work

The calculators use simple, transparent math. Yearly figures assume 52 weeks or 365 days a year, and projections multiply the same habit over one, five or ten years without adding inflation or price changes. The results are estimates meant to give you a clear picture, not financial advice.

The English version shows amounts in US dollars, and the other language versions use their local currency, but the math works the same in any currency – just enter prices in the currency you use. Your settings are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark a calculation or share it with a link.

Private by design

Every calculation happens locally in your browser. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts, and the site sets no cookies. The only things stored on your device are your theme and language choices. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Frequently asked questions

Is costsimulators.com free?

Yes. All tools are completely free, with no sign-up, no paywall and no ads.

Are my numbers saved or sent anywhere?

No. Everything you enter is calculated in your browser and never sent to a server. If you share a link, the settings in that link are visible to whoever opens it.

Can I use the calculators at work?

Yes. Commercial use is allowed, including in meetings, reports, presentations and client work.

How accurate are the results?

The results are as accurate as the numbers you enter. The calculators use straightforward formulas and do not account for taxes, inflation or price changes, so treat long-term projections as estimates.
